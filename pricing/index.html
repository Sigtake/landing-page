Before you sign up

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature listed on this page is on while Sigtake is in early access — no trial clock counting down, no card on file, no feature held back for a paid tier that doesn’t exist yet.

What happens to my data when early access ends?

It stays. Your projects, teams, notification channels, API keys and alert history are not touched when pricing changes, and we announce any change by email before it takes effect.

Is there a limit on how many alerts I can send?

There’s no cap on volume during early access. Each API key is rate-limited — 60 requests a minute by default, up to 1,000 — and deduplication means a flapping check collapses into one incident with an occurrence counter instead of a thousand rows; renotification only fires at 1, 10, 25, 50, 100 and every 100 after that.

Do I need a credit card?

No. There’s no billing in the product today. Registering takes an email address, and your first alert can be in the Alert Center a few minutes later.

Can I move over from the alerting tool I use today?

Sigtake sits downstream of whatever you already run, so there’s no migration to schedule. Point a cron job, a CI step or an SDK call at the ingest API with one key (tools with a fixed webhook format, like Alertmanager, need a small relay) and run both systems in parallel until you trust the routing.

What does Business include that early access doesn’t?

Nothing is feature-gated today. Business is the conversation for organizations that need requirements in writing — such as enforced 2FA or audit exports — and priority support, under a contract rather than a promise on a pricing page. Pricing is agreed per organization.

Something else? Help & support answers within two business days, and the documentation covers setup end to end.
